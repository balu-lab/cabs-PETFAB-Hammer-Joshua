# Lesson 1: Introduction to the Robotic Arm

Students learn the parts and vocabulary of a robotic arm, what degrees of freedom are, and how to work safely around the arm. The lesson frames the whole course around three questions: how the arm knows where it is, how it knows where to go, and how it can interact with the world.

**Time:** one class period (about 60 minutes)

## Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

- Identify and describe the core parts of a robotic arm (actuators, links, encoders, grippers, controller, workspace)
- Define degrees of freedom (DOF) and explain why they matter for an arm's range of motion
- Describe the three questions that guide the course
- State the essential safety procedures for operating the arm
